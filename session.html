★ Conference registration

  • There is no fee for team registration and initial proposal submission to SustAI.
  • Teams that will be shortlisted for the main SustAI session (at the FIRE conference) will need to register with a special discounted registration fee of INR 750 + GST per person. At least one member of every shortlisted team has to register and demo their system in-person at the FIRE conference.
  • This special discounted registration fee is applicable only for the SustAI session, and does not cover any other session of the FIRE conference.
  • Registration for the full FIRE conference includes registration for SustAI. In other words, anyone who registers for the full FIRE conference can submit to SustAI, and does not need to separately register for this session if shortlisted.
