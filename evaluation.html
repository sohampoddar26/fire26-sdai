03 · Evaluation

Useful, relevant,
original.

The review is designed to be inclusive while identifying systems with genuine potential for social or environmental impact.

01

SDG relevance

How clearly does the system contribute to one or more UN Sustainable Development Goals?

02

Potential impact

How important is the problem, and what social or environmental impact could the system create?

03

Practical usability

Can people use the system effectively? We will consider the quality of the user interface and experience.

04

Innovation

What original contribution has the team made in framing or solving a practical problem?

Two requirements

Open tools.
Working product.

All AI technologies used must be open-source and publicly available free of cost.

AI assistants may be used to develop the system, but their use should be clearly described in the system proposal.
